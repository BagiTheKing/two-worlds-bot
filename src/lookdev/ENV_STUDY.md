# The reference's worlds, frame by frame

These notes come from Benly's "Steve tries to SPEEDRUN" (at 2.0, 5.5, 7.0, 9.0, 13, 18.5, 19.5, 26.5, 28 and 51 s) and "MINECRAFT MOVIE trailer" (at 10, 13, 22.5 and 46 s).

**Composition**
- The camera is low, at grass height or at the character's chest. The world fills the frame up to the top third: hills rise behind the character, and the sky is a band at the top.
- The depth of field is shallow. Foreground grass, logs and leaves are big, blurred shapes at the frame edges.
- Every plane has something in it: grass in front, the character, trees and hills, then mountains, then sky and clouds.

**Terrain**
- Hills are built from one-block steps. On a slope the step sides show as brown dirt stripes under a thin green fringe, and those stripes are what make a hill read as Minecraft.
- Grass tops are a vivid yellow-green with pixel texture. Sun-lit slopes go nearly lime, and shaded slopes go olive.
- Variety comes from grey stone cliffs on steep faces, snow on peaks above a line, sand and gravel at the water, and still blue water with reflections.

**Trees**
- Canopies are large, bumpy blobs of leaf blocks, about 5 to 7 blocks across. The surface is irregular, with blocks missing and blocks sticking out.
- Leaves are see-through (the "fancy" kind). Through the holes you see darker leaves inside, so a canopy has depth: lit leaves are bright yellow-green, and the gaps are nearly black-green.
- Pink cherry trees and white birch trunks are colour accents. Forests cover the far hills too.

**Ground cover**
- Grass is dense everywhere: short grass, tall grass and ferns, a few flowers (poppy, dandelion, daisy). Near the camera the blades are large, out of focus, and backlit.

**Sky**
- Deep saturated blue overhead, about RGB (60–95, 165, 255). It turns pale near the horizon, about (195, 220, 250), and goes white-pink close to the sun.
- Clouds are Minecraft's flat blocky cloud layer seen from below: many small white fragments that shrink toward the horizon.

**Light**
- A warm sun, low to medium, often in or near the frame, with a big soft glow and rays.
- Shadows stay bright and cool, never black. Edges of grass, leaves and characters catch golden rim light.
- Distant hills and mountains fade into pale blue haze, and the farthest forests go teal. This aerial perspective gives the depth.

**Grade and particles**
- Saturated, punchy colour with bloom on everything bright.
- Floating specks and drifting leaves.
