# Episode 9: "Before the Finale" (draft for review)

**Purpose:** a gift for the regulars and a catch-up for new viewers. The three
heroes, still in their old look, explain the story so far over the best
moments, joking. Then they get upgraded on screen, one body part at a time.
The world upgrades too. Then Null arrives, upgraded, deletes it all, and they
run. It leads straight into the finale.

**Length:** about 2:30 to 2:50, a long Short. **REALITY:** 12%.
**Title idea:** "Steve & Noob Got UPGRADED Before the Finale" (the frame-1
promise: the upgrade, and Null).

## 0. Hook (0:00–0:04), the new look

The first frame is the thumbnail shot, upgraded: the new Steve, Noob and
Patch running toward the camera, with the new Null rising behind them, blocks
orbiting. A tape-rewind sound plays and the picture rewinds into the old look.

- **NOOB (VO):** "Okay, wait. Before *that* happens..."

The last shot of the episode is this same moment, so the video loops.

## 1. The briefing (0:04–0:20), old look, old meadow

The trio stand in a row. Patch projects a holographic screen (the Ep7 memory
look) beside them.

- **NOOB** (to camera): "Hi, new people! You have no idea what's going on. ...Same."
- **STEVE:** "Eight episodes of problems. Patch. Show them."
- **PATCH:** "Playing the archive. Please keep your arms inside the timeline."

## 3. The theories (1:20–1:40)

- **PATCH:** "The observers have theories."

A comment card pops up on the hologram: **@youssefakram7493:** "one of them
shouldn't be in a world"

- **NOOB:** "One of us shouldn't be in this world..." (He looks at Steve.) "It's you. You're always holding a pickaxe."
- **STEVE:** "You're always *dying*."
- **NOOB:** "...Patch? Which one?"

Patch slowly turns to the camera. His eye narrows to a squint. A beat.

- **PATCH:** "No comment."

Nothing is confirmed (playbook rule).

## 4. The upgrade (1:40–2:15)

- **PATCH:** "Good news. The finale has a budget."
- **NOOB:** "A what?"
- **PATCH:** "Upgrade starting. Please do not move."

Each character upgrades on screen. A scan line of cyan pixels sweeps up the
body, and each part swaps from the old model to the new one with a pixel
burst and a chime: **legs, then body, then hands, then head**.

1. **Steve.** The hair pops in last.
   - **STEVE** (touching it): "...I have hair. Real hair."
   - **NOOB:** "It's beautiful."
   - **STEVE:** "Don't."
2. **The Noob.** The plastic turns into a pixel skin.
   - **NOOB:** "Why do I feel... *textured*?"
   - **STEVE:** "Welcome to Minecraft."
3. **Patch.** The shell, then the screen; the eye makes its first ^ smile.
   - **NOOB:** "PATCH CAN SMILE?!"
   - **PATCH:** "I always could. Now you can see it."
4. **The world.** The camera pulls back. The old flat meadow sweeps away into
   the new world: terraced hills, cherry trees, golden light.
   - **NOOB:** "Woooah."
   - **STEVE:** "Okay. That's... nice."

## 5. Null (2:15–2:45)

- **NOOB:** "Do you think *he* got upgraded too?"

The wind cuts out, then the music: the series' silence device. Total silence.

- **STEVE:** "...Why did it go quiet?"

The new Null rises behind the hill in his storm, blocks orbiting. He deletes
the new world chunk by chunk toward them: the cherry trees and the terraces.

- **PATCH** (calm): "He got upgraded too."
- **NOOB:** "RUUUN!"

They run toward the camera: the thumbnail shot, which loops back to frame 1.

**End card,** an overlay on the last shot:

> **THE FINALE** · The First Portal Was a Trap
>
> **Which one shouldn't be in this world?** 👇
