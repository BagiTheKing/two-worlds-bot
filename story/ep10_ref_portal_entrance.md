The creator's reference is "[MINECRAFT ANIMATION] ROBLOX VS STEVE (EPIC
FIGHT)" by Elchiromon Animations: the scene where the Roblox Noob comes
through a nether portal, at about **0:10–0:36** of that video. The creator
wants Null's entrance to be **as epic as that, with the creep in it**. The
video isn't in the repo (it's someone else's); this is a shot-by-shot study
of it (frames sampled at 3 fps, sound measured every 0.5 s).

## The techniques to steal

1. **Start small and far, end huge and close.** The portal is a tiny glowing
   rectangle; the threat is revealed piece by piece: a hand, arms, the crawl,
   the face last.
2. **The membrane stretches.** The portal surface deforms around the hand
   pushing through it, like skin. That's the single creepiest image.
3. **The camera on the ground** for the approach and the crawl.
4. **Wrong motion:** jerky, claw-like, crawling with the head turned away.
5. **Light:** the low sun backlighting through the trees (god rays, flare),
   with purple portal light from the other side. The figure is rim-lit from
   both sides. The frames are dark but the colour is rich. (Our playbook
   still asks for brightness: keep the sky and the sun bright, and let only
   the forest floor go dark.)
6. **Sound:** the music is loud during the approach and **drops out the
   moment the hands appear** (our series' silence device does exactly
   this). Then one hit on the face reveal, then quiet.
7. **Held reaction shots** between the reveals.

## Fitting it into our finale

The reference takes about 25 s; our whole finale is 40–45 s. Keep the
technique and compress the time to about 7–8 s for the entrance. Rebalance
the rest of the story (the fast-forward can shrink to about 3 s).
