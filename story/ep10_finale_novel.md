# The Finale: "The First Portal Was a Trap"

*Episode 10, told as a story so we can judge it before it becomes a script.*

This is the long version. It contains every beat, every line and every
joke we might want. The Short has to fit in under 3:00, so the beat sheet at
the end marks what is essential and what can go. The spoken lines are written
exactly as they would be voiced. Everything else is narration: the picture,
the sound and the feeling we are aiming for.

It picks up from the last frame of Episode 8: the first portal, hidden in
the forest, switches itself on, and Null's copied smile comes through it.
Episode 9's teaser (the upgraded world, Null rising, the run) happens inside
this episode, so a viewer who watched Episode 9 recognises the moment.

---

## 1. Hi?

The portal had been asleep for a very long time.

Steve had built it before he knew the Noob's name, before the dragon and the
duck and the block that shouldn't exist. He'd hidden it behind a wall of
leaves and tried to forget it. Now it stood open in the middle of the
forest. Its frame hummed with a purple light, and something on the other
side was coming closer.

First a glow. Then a shape. Then, pressing out of the light like a face
against a window, a smile: wide, simple, and painted in magenta and black
squares, the colour of a texture that failed to load.

The Noob knew that smile. He saw it every morning in puddles.

He did what he always did when meeting someone new. He waved.

**NOOB:** "...Hi?"

The smile tilted its head exactly the way he did.

**NULL** *(the Noob's voice, deep and broken, like a speaker underwater)*: "...Hi?"

And then the forest went quiet.

Not quiet the way forests get at night. The wind stopped halfway through a
leaf. A bird opened its beak and nothing came out. Even the music, which
had been following the three of them around all season, stepped back and
held its breath.

**STEVE** *(very low)*: "Patch. Why is it quiet?"

**PATCH** *(calm, polite, the way you'd announce a delayed train)*: "Because he's here."

Null came through.

He didn't walk; he *unfolded*. A dark island of broken blocks climbed out of
the portal, much too big for the frame, and the frame let it happen.
Cubes peeled off his body and drifted around him in slow orbits, and on top
of it all sat the checkered sphere with the Noob's smile. He rose behind
the hill, higher than the trees and the clouds, until the sky behind him
turned purple.

Then he raised one hand, and the cherry trees at the top of the hill turned
into squares of nothing.

---

## 2. We're in the finale

They ran.

This was the shot everyone who watched Episode 9 remembers: the three of
them sprinting toward us across the new, beautiful world while it gets
deleted behind them, chunk by chunk, with the hum of a giant eraser.

**NOOB** *(running, delighted, terrified)*: "We're IN the finale!"

**STEVE:** "Noob! RUN!"

**NOOB:** "I AM running!"

*(Long-time viewers will feel the floor tilt here. That exchange is word for
word the first thing they ever heard these two say, in Episode 0. We are
catching up to the start.)*

In the corner of the screen the REALITY meter, which had been sitting at
12% since Episode 8, began to slide: 11%, 9%, 6%.

Then reality stopped.

It was exactly like Episode 0. A bird froze in the middle of a flap. A
falling leaf hung in the air like a sticker. The three of them kept running
through a world that had been paused, the only moving things in it, while
the deletion followed them without a sound.

Ahead lay a bridge: a thin wooden line across a canyon with no bottom. On
the far side, a ledge.

They were halfway across when the planks in front of them started blinking
out, one at a time, like a loading bar running backwards.

**NOOB:** "Oh! The bridge is under maintenance!"

It was the same line as last time. The bridge didn't laugh last time
either.

The last plank went. They jumped, and they fell, but not far. Two hands, one
square and one round, caught the edge of the ledge, and there they hung over
a void that went down forever.

Patch floated down beside them and stayed just out of reach, which was
typical of Patch.

The REALITY meter landed on **3%**, and stopped.

---

## 3. The ledge

Everything that came next had happened before.

**NOOB** *(looking down)*: "Steve... can you respawn if there's no world?"

**STEVE:** "No."

**NOOB:** "...Oh."

Then Patch did something he had never done on this ledge before. He spoke.

**PATCH:** "We've been here before."

**STEVE:** "What?"

**PATCH:** "This ledge. This exact ledge. You say 'No'. He says 'Oh'. Then we fall."

**NOOB:** "...How do you know?"

**PATCH:** "Because I remember. I'm a glitch detector. You two are the glitch."

He turned his single cyan eye toward the void and projected a picture onto
the darkness. It was the same hologram look as Episode 7's memory, so
returning viewers know they're watching the past.

**PATCH:** "Steve's first portal didn't open a door between your worlds. It opened a hole *under* them."

A hole appeared in the hologram: a dark drain beneath two tiny worlds,
pulling at their edges.

**PATCH:** "When the world ends, everything falls in. And the portal catches it, and spits it out at the start. The forest. Twenty-four hours earlier."

The hologram showed two small figures tumbling out of the purple light and
into the trees. One of them landed face-first next to a tree and said,
very faintly, *"Whoa. Where am I?"*

**STEVE** *(quietly)*: "I built a door."

**PATCH:** "You built a trap."

That's the title of the episode, said by the character who would know.

**STEVE:** "...How many times?"

Patch's eye flickered, which is what it does instead of sighing.

**PATCH:** "I stopped counting. At four hundred and twelve."

The Noob processed this the way the Noob processes everything: by finding
the one detail that matters least.

**NOOB:** "Did I punch the tree every time?"

**PATCH:** "Every time."

**NOOB:** "...And it respected me every time?"

**PATCH:** "Every time."

**NOOB** *(deeply moved)*: "Loyal."

**STEVE:** "Noob."

**NOOB:** "Sorry."

Then Patch showed them the part nobody had wanted to see.

In the hologram, the Noob died. It happened the cheerful, careless way he
always died: a lava jump, a button, a tutorial. Every time he respawned
with a pop and a "New record!", and every time, a small dark cube drifted
away from where he'd fallen. It sank down, down, through the hole under the
world, into the dark.

*(The rewatchers' payoff: those cubes have been hiding in the corner of the
frame since Episode 1.)*

In the void the cubes piled up, four hundred and twelve loops of them,
until the pile opened its eyes.

**PATCH:** "Every time you respawned, your old body fell in there."

**NOOB** *(small)*: "...Where it goes."

**PATCH:** "Where it goes."

**STEVE:** "And that's..."

**PATCH:** "Null."

The Noob looked down at the dark below his dangling sneakers, and for the
first time all season he didn't make a joke.

**NOOB:** "That's why he has my smile."

---

## 4. Reality has been corrected

The music came back, but only to do something terrible.

Null's theme, the one from Episode 0, rose out of the void, and so did
Null. He came up slowly and silently, filling the whole bottom of the
screen, then the middle, until there was nothing behind the two hanging
heroes but checkered magenta and black.

Up close, you could finally see what he was made of. They weren't blocks.
They were thousands of small dark cubes, and each one had a tiny smile.

**NULL:** "Reality... has been corrected."

He lifted one enormous hand toward the ledge, toward their fingers. In
Episode 0 this is where the ledge disappeared. This is where they fell and
the season started over.

**STEVE** *(fast, low, the engineer under pressure)*: "Okay. He's made of you. Every you that ever respawned."

**NOOB:** "Yeah, I got that part."

**STEVE:** "So he knows every move you've ever made. Every jump. Every button. Every 'This one?'"

**PATCH:** "Four hundred and twelve times."

**STEVE:** "That's why we lose. He always knows what you'll do next."

The hand came closer. Its shadow fell across their faces.

**STEVE:** "So do something you have *never* done."

**NOOB:** "Like what?!"

**STEVE:** "I don't know! Something a Noob has never done!"

The Noob scrunched his face up. You could almost hear the gears: a small
plastic engine running at full power for the first time in its life.

**NOOB:** "...Think?"

**STEVE:** "Faster!"

The Noob looked at Null. He didn't look at the hand. He looked at the
cubes, the thousands of little smiles, each one a version of him that had
fallen and been forgotten while he bounced back up and shouted "New
record!" He'd never once looked back to see where they went.

And then he did the one thing no Noob had ever done.

**NOOB** *(quiet, not a joke)*: "Hi, guys."

**NULL** *(copying, automatically)*: "...Hi, guys."

**NOOB:** "I never came back for you. ...I'm sorry."

Null opened his mouth to copy it, because copying is what Null does.

**NULL:** "I'm..."

Nothing came out.

There was nothing to copy. In four hundred and twelve loops no Noob had ever
said sorry, so the word wasn't anywhere inside him.

The checkered face flickered. The smile stuttered: on, off, on. A sound like
a corrupted save file went through him, and the enormous hand stopped one
pixel short of Steve's fingers.

Then the silence broke. A single bird, frozen since the bridge, finished its
flap and sang.

---

## 5. The fragment

Something came loose.

One small dark cube came off Null's chest and drifted upward, slow and
uncertain, the way a balloon leaves a hand. It floated up past the giant
fingers, past the edge of the void, all the way to the Noob.

It had a tiny smile, and it looked scared.

The Noob let go of the ledge with one hand to catch it, because of course
he did. Some things even a finale can't fix.

His other hand slipped.

**STEVE:** "NOOB!"

Steve has never shouted, not really, not in the whole season. "Hide!"
doesn't count, and "Run!" doesn't count. This one counts: the one shout the
character is allowed, saved for when it matters.

Steve grabbed him. He didn't use a tool, a block or a plan. He used a bare
square hand around a round yellow wrist.

They hung there: Steve holding the ledge, Steve holding the Noob, the Noob
holding one small frightened cube against his chest.

**NOOB** *(dangling, amazed)*: "...No tool?"

**STEVE** *(deadpan, through his teeth)*: "I'm a Roblox player."

*(Episode 1's line, given back. That's the biggest laugh we have, so we
don't step on it.)*

Below them Null wavered. With his smile still glitching, he sank back into
the void, slowly, the way someone sits down after hearing bad news. The
purple light folded in after him, and the void closed like an eye.

Far away, in what was left of the forest, a portal frame hidden behind a
wall of leaves flickered once and went dark.

---

## 6. Three percent

Steve pulled them up.

They lay on their backs on the ledge for a moment, breathing, the way people
do in movies. Then they sat up and looked around at what was left.

It was an island floating in the dark: one patch of grass, one cherry tree
dropping pink leaves into nothing, a few blocks of dirt underneath, and
around it a void as still as a held breath. That was all the world there
was. Three percent.

**STEVE** *(narrating, the way he opened Episode 0)*: "There used to be two worlds. Now there's one tree..."

**NOOB** *(holding up the cube)*: "...one cube..."

**STEVE:** "...and him."

**NOOB** *(waving at us)*: "Hi!"

A cheerful chime rang out, and the pop-up from Episode 0 slid onto the
screen. Last time it appeared while they were falling, which was a lie.
This time it's true:

> **ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED · SURVIVE THE APOCALYPSE**
> *0.1% of players have this*

**NOOB** *(fists in the air)*: "IT'S REAL THIS TIME!"

**STEVE:** "Don't celebrate on the edge."

**NOOB:** "Everything is the edge!"

He had a point.

Quiet came back, the good kind. They sat with their legs over the side of
the world, and the Noob turned the little cube over in his hands. Its smile
had stopped glitching. It looked like it was napping.

**NOOB:** "Hey, Steve. The first time you opened the portal... what did you see? On the other side."

Steve was quiet for a moment, long enough for a cherry leaf to fall past
them and into the dark.

**STEVE:** "Someone who fell off everything. And got back up laughing."

**NOOB** *(slowly realising, hand on heart)*: "...Aww."

**STEVE:** "Don't."

*(The "It's beauti— / Don't." rhythm from Episode 9. Steve stays Steve.)*

Patch floated beside them, and his eye did something it had never done
before: it looked unsure.

**STEVE:** "Patch. What happens now?"

**PATCH:** "I don't know."

**NOOB:** "You always know!"

**PATCH:** "I always *remembered*. Four hundred and twelve times, I knew exactly what came next." *(his eye curves into its small ^ smile)* "This is the first time I don't."

Then Patch turned and looked straight into the camera, the way he did in
Episode 6 and Episode 7 when nobody knew who he was talking to. Now,
maybe, we do.

**PATCH:** "...Someone kept watching."

In the corner of the screen the REALITY meter, stuck at 3% for the whole
season, flickered and changed. Not to a number, to a question mark:

> **REALITY: ?**

---

## 7. The stinger

The music faded. The island glowed softly in the dark. The Noob held the
cube up to his face and smiled at it, and the cube smiled back, a tiny
copy of his.

Then, for half a second, its smile flickered magenta.

The sound went out. Not the music: every sound, all at once, the series'
total silence.

From very far below, out of the void that was supposed to be closed, came a
voice. It was the Noob's voice, pitched all the way down and broken:

**NULL:** "You weren't supposed to survive."

Over the last shot, without cutting to black, the end strip slides in:

> **SEASON 2**
> **Who built the trap?**
> *Comment your theory*

The last frame is the dark void with one magenta glow in the middle. It
flows straight back into the first frame, the glowing portal in the forest,
so the episode loops: the trap always opens again.

# Notes for review

## What the finale answers (and what it keeps)

| Season question | End-card episode | The finale's answer |
|---|---|---|
| Who is Null? | Ep 0 | The Noob's old bodies, four hundred and twelve loops of them |
| What was watching them? | Ep 2 | Null, from the other side (the mirror that waved back) |
| Who froze the dragon? | Ep 3 | Null, "correcting" (implied, never said) |
| Why did it copy him? | Ep 6 | It's made of him |
| How many times? | Ep 7 | 412, then Patch stopped counting |
| What did Steve see on the other side? | Ep 8 | "Someone who fell off everything. And got back up laughing." |
| Which one shouldn't be in this world? | Ep 9 | **Kept open.** The finale never confirms a viewer theory |
| Who is measuring REALITY? | (bible) | **Kept open.** Patch only says "Someone kept watching" |

New questions for Season 2:
- **Who built the trap?** Steve built the portal, but Null's last line says
  someone *expected* them to fall.
- **The cube:** what is the fragment they saved, and why did its smile
  flicker?
- **REALITY: ?** It's no longer a number.

## Why the win makes sense

- Null wins every loop because he is made of every Noob who ever lived those
  loops. He knows every move the Noob can make.
- The only thing that can beat him is a move that has never happened. The
  Noob's careless trait all season (he never thinks about where the old
  bodies go) becomes the answer: he finally looks back and says sorry.
  Null copies everything, but he has nothing to copy for that word, so he
  breaks.
- Steve's arc closes alongside it. In Episode 8 he admitted he built the
  portal. In the finale he refuses to let the Noob fall into it: no tool, no
  plan, just his hand, and his one real shout.
- Both of them win by owning a mistake. The comedy stays on top of it
  ("Loyal.", "I'm a Roblox player.", "Don't.").

## The bible and playbook, checked

- **Frame 1 is the title's promise:** a portal with a monster coming through
  it, readable by a stranger. It also continues Episode 8's last frame.
- **First line at about 0.2 s:** "...Hi?" A stranger understands it
  instantly, and it's creepy when the reply comes back.
- **One premise, a build-up, one twist:** the trap is explained, the hand
  comes, the sorry.
- **Spectacle:**
  - Null unfolding out of the portal;
  - the deletion chase and the frozen world;
  - the bridge;
  - Null made of a thousand smiling cubes, seen up close;
  - the 3% island in the void.
- **The silence device** is used three ways: Null arrives (silence), the
  sorry breaks it (a bird sings), and the stinger brings total silence
  again.
- **No new idea in the last seconds:**
  - the cube was planted since Episode 1;
  - Patch's look at the camera was set up in Episodes 6 and 7;
  - Null's voice has been heard since Episode 0.
- **Cartoon stakes only:** the "old bodies" are small dark cubes with little
  smiles, never corpses.

## Decisions for you

1. **"Null entering the portal."** I read Episode 8's end as Null coming
   *through* the first portal into their world (Episode 8's staging: the
   portal lights by itself and the smile comes closer through it). If you
   meant Null *going into* the portal, the opening changes but the rest
   holds.
2. **The number 412.** It's specific and funny. We can change it, or keep
   "I stopped counting" with no number.
3. **The sorry.** It's the emotional centre. If it feels too soft for the
   channel, the alternative is a comedy version of the same logic: the Noob
   does nothing at all ("For once in your life... do NOTHING."). Null
   copies stillness, freezes, and the hand stops. It's funnier, but it says
   less.
4. **Steve's one shout:** "NOOB!" at the slip. Episode 9's "STOP TALKING AND
   RUN!" was loud, but it was played as a joke.
5. **The ending stack** is long: the island, the achievement, "what did you
   see", "I don't know", "someone kept watching", "?" and the stinger. For
   the Short I'd keep the achievement, "Don't.", "I don't know", "?" and the
   stinger, and drop the narration bookend.
6. **The Season 2 question:** "Who built the trap?" (my pick), or "What is
   the cube?"
7. **The YouTube title.** "The First Portal Was a Trap" matches frame 1.
   Another option with a stronger promise: "Steve & Noob vs Null: THE FINALE".
